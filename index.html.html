0kay so whats actually happening

So basically theres this earth just floating there and 4 other planets orbiting near it. mars, a saturn (has a ring, i was proud of that one), some purple striped guy i just called "gas guy" cause i couldnt think of a name, and a little grey moon rock that looks more like a party hat honestly.

Click on any of them and earth eats it. It flashes a random color, wobbles around a bit, and its texture gets more scribbly the more it eats.

  • Click a floating planet -> earth eats it
  • Hover over one and it bounces a bit, small detail but i like it
  • Drag around with your mouse to look at stuff (orbit controls did the heavy lifting here)
  • Spin button = earth spins faster, click again for slow again

Random facts (some made up)

1. Earth doesnt actually get bigger when it eats a planet, it just pretends really hard.

2. The moon rock isnt technically from our moon. dont l00k into it too much.

3. That glowy diagonal band you might notice in the background is just dots painted onto a canvas texture, its not a real photo of space or anything.

4. Saturns ring here is one (1) torus shape. the real saturn probably has more going on but i only had time for one.

5. I built this in a browser tab while another tab had music playing, if that matters to you for some reason.
